Community Book Haven

Welcome

A welcoming space where the community can discover books and share knowledge.
Our purpose is to provide a quiet, resource-rich environment that supports academic success, professional development, and lifelong learning for everyone in the neighbourhood.
Whether you are a student researching for a project or a lifelong learner seeking new ideas, we provide the tools and spaces you need to thrive.

Library Environment

Our library is designed to give a conducive, comfortable and distraction free environment for the community members

Recommended Learning Resources

To support your research and skill-building beyond our physical collection, we recomend looking up external educational platforms. Visit our partner website to thousands of free courses and tutorials

  • For Academic Books and Research, use our MYLOFT Digital Library
  • For Skill-Building and Tutorials Visit the Khan Academy Learning Portal for thousands of free courses and tutorials.

Steps of Becoming a Community Book Haven Member

  1. Visit our reception desk during library operating hours.
  2. Provide a valid form of identification such as National ID or Student ID.
  3. Complete the membership application form with your contact details.
  4. Receive your official membership card to begin your learning and growing journey with us.

Library Resources and Services

  • Free high-speed WiFi for research and digital projects.
  • Dedicated focused study areas for focused learning.
  • Printing and scanning services for student assignments.
  • Community workshops and peer to peer tutoring sessions.
  • Access to a wide collection of academic an·d recreational books.
